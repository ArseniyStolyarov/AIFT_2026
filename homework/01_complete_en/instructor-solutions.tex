\heading{Homework 1 · Instructor solutions}{Lectures 1 and 2 · Parts A and B}
\paragraph{Assessment.} Part A is a 120-minute formative mock, marked out of 100 for diagnosis only: mistakes do not reduce the final course grade. Part B is the ordinary assessed homework, redesigned as an instructional training set, also out of 100. Do not add or average the two scores. The three assessed homeworks remain equally weighted within the 20\% homework component. Both parts are handwritten. The submission deadline is 14 November 2026.
\paragraph{Timing.} The selected Part A is demanding for 120 minutes: ten claims, two multi-part problems and two three-sentence answers. Treat completion and where a student runs out of time as diagnostic information. Do not require a textbook-length proof when a concise valid argument suffices. Part B has no time limit and now contains only three compulsory exercises, worth 30, 35 and 35 points. Four additional exercises appear at the end of the same student assignment, without points or submission requirements. Their solutions are at the end of this key; do not require or mark them.
\paragraph{Part A marking.} Ten true/false claims: 5 points each, normally 1 for the verdict and 4 for a valid justification; a bare unsupported label earns zero as announced. Problem A2 subparts (a)--(e): 2, 4, 4, 2, 3 points. Problem A3 subparts (a)--(e): 4, 5, 8, 4, 4 points. Short answers: 5 each. Give partial credit for valid intermediate reasoning and accept equivalent counterexamples.
\paragraph{True/false key.} F, T, T, F, F; T, F, F, F, T.
