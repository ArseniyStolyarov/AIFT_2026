\section*{A4. Two short answers · 10 points}
Answer \emph{each} question in no more than three sentences. Each question is worth 5 points. Treat these as hypothetical cases; no knowledge of a particular news article is required.
\subsection*{1. Paying to resist temptation}
A person pays for an app that blocks social media, even though they could simply choose not to open it for free.

Does paying to remove an available option necessarily reveal irrational preferences? Explain which assumption of the standard consumer-choice model matters for your answer.
\subsection*{2. Too cheap to be desirable}
A shopper wants a bag costing 100,000 rubles but says they would lose interest if the same model cost 10,000 rubles and became widely available.

Does this contradict rational consumer choice? Explain what the shopper might be consuming besides the bag's physical characteristics and what would need to remain unchanged for a meaningful comparison.
