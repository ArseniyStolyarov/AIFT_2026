\section*{Mock 4. Two short answers · 10 points}
Answer each question in \textbf{no more than three sentences}. Each is worth
5 points. These are hypothetical cases; no outside news knowledge is needed.
\subsection*{1. The inflation-proof stipend}
After a price change, a scholarship administrator adjusts a student's
stipend so that the student's old optimal basket is exactly affordable.
Preferences and the available goods stay unchanged.

Does this adjustment preserve \emph{exactly} the old utility? Explain how
it differs from Hicksian compensation.
\subsection*{2. The warranty and the raffle}
A person buys a warranty priced above the expected repair cost and also
a raffle ticket costing 1, paying 10 with probability $0.05$ and zero
otherwise. The ticket is independent of existing risks and has no
entertainment or other nonmonetary benefit; final wealth stays positive.

Can one strictly increasing, globally concave expected-utility index of wealth
explain both purchases? Identify an assumption that could be changed to
explain the behaviour.
