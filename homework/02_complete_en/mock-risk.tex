\section*{Mock 3. Keeping the emergency fund alive · 20 points}
A freelancer has wealth 144 and faces a loss of 128 with probability $1/2$.
Bernoulli utility is $b(w)=\sqrt w$. Partial insurance pays coverage I only
if the loss occurs; its premium $2I/3$ is paid in either state, and
$0\le I\le128$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item \textbf{4 points.} Without insurance, find expected wealth, expected
utility, the certainty equivalent and risk premium.
\item \textbf{4 points.} What is the largest \emph{lump-sum premium} the
freelancer would pay for full insurance? Compare it with the expected loss.
\item \textbf{4 points.} Write final wealth in both states and the
expected-utility objective for partial coverage. Establish strict concavity.
\item \textbf{6 points.} Find optimal coverage, check the interval bounds
and calculate wealth in both states at the optimum.
\item \textbf{2 points.} Calculate the certainty equivalent at optimal
coverage. Does accepting a lower expected final wealth here contradict
expected-utility maximisation?
\end{enumerate}
