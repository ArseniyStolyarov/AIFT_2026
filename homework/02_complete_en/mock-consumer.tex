\section*{Mock 2. Two goods, one welfare bill · 20 points}
For $x\in\R_+^2$, utility is
\[
 u(x)=\frac{x_1x_2}{x_1+x_2}\quad\text{when }x_1+x_2>0,
 \qquad u(0,0)=0.
\]
You may use the indirect utility function without proving it:
\[
 v(p,m)=\frac{m}{(\sqrt{p_1}+\sqrt{p_2})^2},\qquad p\gg0,\ m>0.
\]
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item \textbf{5 points.} Use Roy's identity to recover both demands and
verify budget exhaustion.
\item \textbf{5 points.} Use duality to find $e(p,\bar u)$ for $\bar u>0$.
Recover both Hicksian demands using Shephard.
\item \textbf{6 points.} Income is 8 and prices change from $(1,1)$ to
$(4,1)$. Calculate initial and final utility, CV and EV. Report the two
welfare measures as positive monetary losses.
\item \textbf{4 points.} Calculate the Marshallian consumer-surplus loss
\[
 L_{CS}=\int_1^4 x_1((s,1),8)\,ds.
\]
Compare it with CV and EV. A useful substitution is $t=\sqrt s$.
\end{enumerate}
